Copywriting Frameworks Guide & FAQ

What is the PAS framework?

Problem-Agitate-Solve: Identifies a specific pain point, agitates the emotional frustration of living with it, and positions your solution as the immediate antidote. It is the highest-converting framework for cold traffic.

What words trigger Meta/TikTok ad policy rejections?

Ad platforms penalize exaggerated claims and unrealistic guarantees. Words such as "guarantee", "miracle", "cure", "get rich", "instant fix", and "100% risk free" frequently flag automated moderation bots. Our linter highlights these in red.

How does BAB (Before-After-Bridge) convert?

BAB contrasts your customer's current painful status quo ("Before") with an idealized future state ("After"), then presents your offer as the direct vehicle ("Bridge") connecting the two worlds.
